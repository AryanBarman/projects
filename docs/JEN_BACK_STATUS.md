## 5. How to continue in a new session
1. Read `HANDOFF.md`, then this file, then RUNBOOK 9 to 9h.
2. To change a beat: edit `tools\jb\<id>_scene.js` (positions in station coordinates; keep labels within about 8 units of the station centre and above y 4), then `python build_jb.py <id>`; re-run `python cam_upgrade.py` / `python dress.py` only if you re-ran a `mk_<id>.py` (they rewrite the cfg).
3. Check a part: `cp jen_back/<out>.html <TESTDIR>/pv_x.html; cd tools/jb; SCR=<scratch> node fitcheck.js pv_x.html <dur> 1`; render stills with `tools\cdp.js`. Always LOOK at 10 to 16 frames per part after a change: the checks cannot see missing or weak assets.
4. Working rules from the user: no false claims, report what was not verified; keep camera moving and labels readable; keep colours bright; the user wants assets that show the idea, not talking characters; do the whole series check at the end and keep improving it.
